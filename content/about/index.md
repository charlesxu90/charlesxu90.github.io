I am a Research Scientist (Postdoctoral Research Fellow) at the
[King Abdullah University of Science and Technology (KAUST)](https://www.kaust.edu.sa/),
in the Computer, Electrical and Mathematical Sciences and Engineering (CEMSE) Division.

My research centers on **agentic AI systems for scientific discovery**, **protein
design**, and **AI for synthetic biology**. I work on enzyme and cyclic peptide
design through collaborative projects that optimize industrial enzymes, and over
the course of my career I have explored chemical design, antibody design, peptide
design, phylogenetics, cancer genomics, and synthetic biology.

📫 **Email:** xiaopeng.xu *{at}* kaust.edu.sa

## Research Interests

- Agentic AI systems for scientific discovery
- Generative models for protein, enzyme, peptide, and antibody design
- AI for synthetic biology
- Cancer genomics, phylogenetics, and bioinformatics
