要点：

* 不能遗漏意见

* 分主次，稳住 positive、争取 negative

* 不懂问题的常见处理方法：

   * Novelty：梳理重要贡献，强调 motivation 和 intuition。或强调简单有效。

   * Factual error：大方承认并感谢

   * 涨点不足：比方法差异，比 reference 证据，

## 常用句式

以下列举一些 rebuttal中 的常用句式，供大家选择使用：

### Editor 邮件回复

#### 版本一

Dear Prof. XXX,

Thank you very much for your letter and the comments from the reviewers about our paper submitted to XXXX (MS Number XXXX). We have checked the manuscript and revised it according to the comments. We submit here the revised manuscript as well as a list of changes. If you have any question about this paper, please don’t hesitate to let me know. 

Sincerely yours,


#### 版本二

Firstly, we would like to thank you for your kind letter and for reviewers’ constructive comments concerning our article (Manuscript No.:XXXXX). These comments are all valuable and helpful for improving our article. All the authors have seriously discussed about all these comments. According to the reviewers’comments, we have tried best to modify our manuscript to meet with the requirements of your journal. In this revised version, changes to our manuscript within the document were all highlighted by using red colored text. Point-by-point responses to the reviewers are listed below this letter.

#### 版本三

On behalf of all the contributing authors, I would like to express our sincere appreciations of your letter and reviewers’ constructive comments concerning our article entitled “XXXát” (Manuscript No.:XXXX). These comments are all valuable and helpful for improving our article. According to the associate editor and reviewers’ comments,we have made extensive modifications to our manuscript and supplemented extra data to make our results convincing. In this revised version, changes to ourmanuscript were all highlighted within the document by using red colored text. Point-by-pointresponses to the nice associate editor and two nice reviewers are listed below this letter.


### Reviewer 答复开头【期刊不适用？】

1. We sincerely thank the reviewer for their time and efforts. Below please find the responses to some specific comments.

2. We thank the useful suggestions from the reviewers. Some important or common questions are first addressed, followed by answers to individual reviews.

3. Thank you for your kind comments on ourmanuscript entitled “át”. We have carefully revised the manuscript according to the Reviewer’s comments. Based on the suggestions, we have made an extensive modification on the revised manuscript. Detailed revision was shown as follows. The changes to our manuscript within the document were aslo highlighted by using red colored text.

4. We feel great thanks for your professional review work on our article. As you are concerned, there are several problems that need to be addressed. According to your nice suggestions, we have made extensive corrections to our previous draft, the detailed corrections are listed below.

5. 

### Comments 逐条回复


#### 礼貌术语

1. We are very sorry for our negligence of ……...

2. We are very sorry for our incorrect writing ……...

3. It is really true as Reviewer suggested that……

4. We have made correction according to the Reviewer’s comments.

5. We have re-written this part according to the Reviewer’s suggestion

6. As Reviewer suggested that……

7. Considering the Reviewer’s suggestion, we have ……

8. The authors appreciate the reviewer’s comments on this manuscript. ……

9. Following the reviewer’s suggestion, we ……

#### 表达同意

1. We thank the reviewer for pointing out this issue.

2. We agree with you and have incorporated this suggestion throughout our paper. We have reflected this comment by …

3. We can/will add/compare/revise/correct ... in our revised manuscript/our final version. 

4. Due to the rebuttal policy, “authors should not include new experimental results in the rebuttal”, additional results may not be included. However, we will add these mentioned experiments and discussions in our final version. Thank you for the constructive comment.（对于 CVPR/ICCV/ECCV rebuttal 不能提供新结果的政策）

#### 表达不同意

1. We respectfully disagree with Reviewer #id that ...

2. The reviewer might have overlooked Table #id ...

3. We can compare ... but it is not quite related to our work ...

4. We have to emphasize that ...

5. The reviewer raises an interesting concern. However, our work ...

6. Thank you for the comment, but we cannot fully agree with the comment. As stated/emphasized ...

7. You have raised an important point; however, we believe that ... would be outside the scope of our paper because …

8. This is a valid assessment of …; however, we believe that ... would be more appropriate because ...

#### 解释澄清

1. We have indeed stated/included/discussed/compared/reported/clarified/elaborated ... in our original paper ... (cf. Line #id).

2. As we stated in Line #id, ...We have rewritten ... to be more in line with your comments. We hope that the edited section clarifies …

#### 语法错误

1. We were really sorry for your careless mistakes. Thank you for your reminding.

2. We tried our best to improve the manuscript and made some changes in the manuscript. These changes will not influence the content and framework of the paper. And here we did not list the changes but marked in red in revised paper.  We appreciate for Editors/Reviewers’ warm work earnestly, and hope that the correction will meet with approval. Once again, thank you very much for your comments and suggestions.

3. Following the reviewer’s suggestion, we modified these phrases in the revised manuscript (please see page 1, line 2 and page 2, line 14 & 15).

4. Thanks for pointing out these errors. We corrected them in the revised manuscript.

#### Reference 错误

1. Thanks for your nice suggestions. We have formatted all the references.


#### 额外信息与解释

1. We have included a new figure/table (cf. Figure/Table #id) to further illustrate…

2. We have supplemented the xxx section with explanations of ...

3. Thank you for the comment. We will explore this in future work.

4. Besides the above changes, we have corrected some expression errors.


### Reviewer 感谢

7. Thank you for the positive/detailed/constructive comments.

8. Special thanks to you for your good comments.

9. Thanks. We have corrected these mistakes based on your suggestions.

10. Thank you again for your positive comments and valuable suggestions to improve the quality of our manuscript.
